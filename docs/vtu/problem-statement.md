# Problem Statement

**Status: software framing complete; community wording to be confirmed with stakeholders (Phase 9).**

People in the community receive scam SMS/WhatsApp messages, OTP-fraud
calls, fake-bank KYC scares, digital-arrest threats and phishing links,
and often act before they can verify. Existing advice is easy to ignore
in the moment, and there is no local, explainable, privacy-respecting
place to check a message, learn the patterns, and report what arrived.

CyberShield Community answers: *check before you trust* — analyze first,
understand why something looks suspicious, act safely, and (optionally
and anonymously) help the community track patterns.
