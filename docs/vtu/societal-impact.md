# Societal Impact

**Status: mechanism built; impact unmeasured (honestly stated).**

What the system *can* do for society: give ordinary people an immediate,
understandable second opinion on suspicious messages/links; teach scam
patterns in plain language; accumulate an anonymous community picture;
give field teams measurable (if narrow) learning signals.

What is **not** claimed: reduced scam incidence, protected participants,
verified intelligence, or any percentage improvement. The design
deliberately separates **system usage** (loggable now) from **awareness
change** (measurable in Phase 9) from **real-world scam reduction**
(requires independent longitudinal data — out of scope).

> **[FIELD EVIDENCE REQUIRED before any impact sentence enters the report]**
